FIRE milestones
Progress toward each level of financial independence, based on your current invested assets. Targets use the standard 4% safe-withdrawal rule (25× annual expenses) — a common planning assumption, not a guarantee. Figures are nominal dollars (see the "today's dollars" metric above for an inflation-adjusted comparison), no state income tax is modeled, and the FIRE targets themselves don't reflect early-withdrawal penalties (those are applied separately, in-year, when 401k/IRA funds are actually drawn before 59½). This is a single deterministic projection — it compounds at one constant return every year with no market downturns, recessions, or sequence-of-returns risk, and it doesn't model capital-gains tax or ordinary income tax on investment withdrawals. A real-world outcome will look choppier and, after taxes, lower than this line.
